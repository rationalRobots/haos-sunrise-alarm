# Sunrise Alarm

A gentle, per-person wake routine for Home Assistant. Some minutes before your
phone alarm, your bedside lamp fades up like a sunrise and (optionally) a speaker
fades in music or radio. Snooze and dismiss from a button or a service.

Configured entirely from the UI — add one profile per sleeper. The routine only
fires for genuine clock-app alarms inside your morning window, so calendar
reminders and routines won't set it off.
